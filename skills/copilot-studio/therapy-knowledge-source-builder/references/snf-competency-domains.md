# SNF Competency Domains — Cross-Discipline Mapping

The 8 unified SNF practice domains used across all knowledge source documents. Consistent naming is critical — scenario domain tags MUST match these exactly.

---

## Domain 1: Clinical Reasoning & Evidence-Based Practice

**PT:** Knowledge for Practice (KP 1-4) + Patient and Client Care (PC 4)  
**OT:** Critical Reasoning  
**SLP:** Clinical Judgment / Evidence-Based Practice

*Covers:* Differential diagnosis, evidence appraisal, clinical decision-making, integrating research into practice, recognizing patterns across complex presentations.

---

## Domain 2: Patient Management & Intervention

**PT:** Patient and Client Care and Services (PC 1-3, 5-13, 15, 17)  
**OT:** Performance Skills  
**SLP:** Assessment & Treatment Planning / Intervention

*Covers:* Examination, evaluation, diagnosis, prognosis, intervention delivery, outcome measurement, modifying treatment based on patient response, recognizing urgent situations.

---

## Domain 3: Communication & Interpersonal Skills

**PT:** Communication (CO 1-2)  
**OT:** Interpersonal Skills  
**SLP:** Counseling & Caregiver Education

*Covers:* Verbal/nonverbal/written communication, cultural humility, adapting strategies to audience, patient/family education, interdisciplinary team communication, SBAR, handoff.

---

## Domain 4: Documentation

**PT:** Practice Management (PM 8-9) + Patient Care (PC 14)  
**OT:** Performance Skills (documentation component)  
**SLP:** Documentation / Record-Keeping

*Covers:* Evaluation reports, daily notes, progress notes, discharge summaries, MDS Section GG/K coding, SMART goals, skilled service justification, compliance with Medicare/regulatory requirements, PHI/HIPAA.

---

## Domain 5: Safety, Ethics & Professionalism

**PT:** Professionalism (PR 1-8)  
**OT:** Ethical Practice  
**SLP:** Professional Ethics / Patient Safety

*Covers:* Code of Ethics, regulatory compliance, cultural competency, mandatory reporting, informed consent, infection control, fall prevention, error reporting, professional boundaries, scope of practice.

---

## Domain 6: Education & Teaching

**PT:** Education and Learning (EL 1-6)  
**OT:** Performance Skills (education component)  
**SLP:** Patient/Family/Caregiver Education

*Covers:* Patient/caregiver education, teaching strategies across learning domains, assessing learning needs, teach-back method, modifying education based on comprehension, mentoring students/ new graduates.

---

## Domain 7: Practice Management & Systems

**PT:** Practice Management (PM 1-3, 6-7) + Systems-Based Practice (SB 1-6)  
**OT:** Ethical Practice (system-level)  
**SLP:** Systems Knowledge / Caseload Management

*Covers:* PDPM classification, MDS coding, caseload management, productivity, supervision of assistants/aides, QAPI, resource allocation, social determinants of health, regulatory environment.

---

## Domain 8: Reflective Practice & Professional Development

**PT:** Reflective Practice and Improvement (RP 1-2)  
**OT:** Knowledge (lifelong learning component)  
**SLP:** Continued Competency Review / Professional Development

*Covers:* Self-assessment, continuing education, competency review, mentorship, clinical specialization, responding to feedback, adapting to evolving evidence.

---

## Domain Tagging Convention

```
**Domain:** Clinical Reasoning & EBP

```

## Proficiency Levels

| Level | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **Novice** | Entry-level — requires direct supervision, follows protocols, limited situational awareness |
| **Advanced Beginner** | Developing — needs occasional guidance, recognizes patterns, some independent decision-making |
| **Competent** | Independent practitioner — manages typical caseload, plans and prioritizes effectively |
| **Proficient** | Advanced — manages complex cases, serves as resource, intuitive grasp of situations |
| **Expert** | Mastery — effortless performance, innovative problem-solving, teaches and mentors others |
