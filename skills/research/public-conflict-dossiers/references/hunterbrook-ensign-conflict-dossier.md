# Hunterbrook / Ensign Public Conflict Dossier Pattern

Session-derived reference for future short-seller/media conflict dossiers. Keep this as a model pattern, not as a substitute for re-checking current sources.

   - Also disclosed law-firm collaboration, including Hunterbrook Law, to pursue reform based on reporting.

2. Subject's methodology/legal disclaimer
   - Positions may change at any time.
   - Media and Capital are legally separate but under common control.
   - Capital may trade before/after publication; article may not be updated for later position changes.
   - Methodology stated numbers were estimates, CMS data may have gaps, CMS data is routinely updated, and findings reflect experiment design choices.
   - Disclaimer provided information "as is" without guarantee of accuracy, timeliness, completeness, reliability, or fitness for purpose.

3. Subject's About page

## Safe argument stack

1. Start with own disclosures: short/long exposure, common control, position-change flexibility.
2. Add business model: media + affiliated fund + litigation monetization.
3. Add methodology caveats: estimates, CMS gaps, design choices.
4. Add market/litigation amplification: stock drop coverage and law-firm solicitations.
5. Add third-party credibility concerns as context.
6. Add target/company counterpoints.
7. Close with independent verification demand, not a falsity accusation.

## Counsel-safe phrasing

Strong:
- "Hunterbrook disclosed a financial conflict at publication: its affiliated fund was short ENSG and long comparable securities."
- "Hunterbrook disclosed that Media and Capital are legally separate entities under common control."
- "Hunterbrook disclosed that positions may change and that the article may not be updated to reflect later changes."
- "Hunterbrook's own methodology characterized key numbers as estimates based on CMS data that may have gaps and research design choices."
- "The conflict does not prove the report false, but it is material context for evaluating motive, neutrality, and market impact."

Avoid:
- "Hunterbrook committed fraud."
- "Hunterbrook manipulated the market."
- "The reporters lied."
- Any personal/private-life material.

## Report structure that worked

- Title/date/scope/boundary/status
- TL;DR with 10 strongest bullets
- Source table
- Numbered findings with quote/paraphrase, why it matters, safe wording, source
- Clean argument stack
- Recommended safe language
- Avoid language
- What was not found / should not use
- Bottom line

## Verification pattern

After writing, verify with file existence plus content search for mandatory anchors:
- short position
- common control
- positions may change
- law-firm collaboration
- estimates / CMS gaps / design choices
- "as is" / no guarantee
- third-party criticism names
- target rebuttal/counterpoints
